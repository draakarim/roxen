Background

Arsalan Karim is a physician-turned-enterprise architect with over two decades of experience spanning healthcare systems, financial services, and digital transformation. As founder of Roxen Dynamics, he developed the SCAR Framework to help organizations align strategy, capabilities, architecture, and risk into cohesive, executable roadmaps.

The Approach

One Framework. Four dimensions. All Connected.

Most organizations treat strategy, capabilities, architecture, and risk as separate problems. They're not. Weakness in strategy cascades into capability gaps. Capability gaps break architecture. Broken architecture explodes risk.

Our framework flips this. We connect all four, so strength in one multiplies across all others.
